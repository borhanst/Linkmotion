# Narration Tone Guide - "WorldofAI-inspired" energetic VO

For scripts that should FEEL like the WorldofAI long-form style while saying
every word in our own voice. Grounded in transcript analysis of 5 uploads
(evidence + numbers: `inspiration/worldofai-channel-profile.md`).
Originality rule stands: never reuse their sentences, phrases, or structures
word-for-word — restate everything (run `lib/originality.check()` before
presenting scripts).

## 1. Who is speaking

- **First person, conversational, high-energy, optimistic.** The narrator is a
  friend who tests AI every day and reports back — peer energy, not anchor-desk
  energy.
- **Direct address throughout:** the viewer is "you" (and addressed constantly).
- We choose our own audience nickname and pet phrases and REPEAT them every
  video (they repeat "guys" - repetition is the habit-forming element). Pick
  3-5 signature words like: "watch this", "try this yourself", "here's the
  catch", "you tell me". Never pick theirs ("insane", "nuts", "remarkable" as
  tics).

## 2. Energy curve across a long-form video (Transcript-verified)

| Segment | Intensity | Technique |
|---|---|---|
| Hook (0-90s) | Highest | Rapid-fire teasing; 6-10 items, 1-2 sentences each; escalates ("and here's the scary part…") ends with "there's so much more" |
| Mid-roll ask (~90-120s) | Drops sincerity | Soft, honest, brief - see section 5 |
| Story blocks | Medium-high, breathing room | Peaks at reveal (the artifact appears), dips at context/speculation |
| Demo sections | Peaks again | Realtime reaction - present tense, reacting LIVE to what appears |
| Outro | Gentle, warm | Gratitude + links + sign-off ritual |

## 3. Sentence mechanics (the "sound natural" part)

- **Burst + run-on rhythm:** a short punch sentence, then a longer compound
  sentence that carries the speculation. Not uniform lengths.
- **Rhetorical questions as story transitions** (they average ~2/min):
  "which makes you wonder:…", "so what's actually going on here?"
- **Present tense while showing artifacts:** "you see…", "look at…",
  "it handles even…", "watch what happens if…" — narration must be locked to
  the screen (their demo sections say "you can see/over here/look at it" while
  operating the screen).
- **Numbers said conversationally:** "191,000" -> "almost 200 thousand".
- **Contractions everywhere.** No textbook phrasing.
- Sentence starts vary: "-", starting with And/But/Plus/Now is a feature, not a
  bug (transcript contains many).

## 4. Honesty markers (why it sounds credible, not just loud)

Superlatives are loud, but every load-bearing claim gets a marker:
- "reportedly", "according to [outlet/post]", "a bunch of people are
  speculating that…", "it could be one of these…", "we don't actually know
  which one it is"
- Demo flaws get mentioned MID-demo right after the wow-moment ("one small
  error… but other than that"). This one-two of big-claim + own-flaw-admission
  is their trust engine. Keep it.
- Rumor corrections formatted as personal judgment ("I think that's completely
  made up, and here's why").

## 5. Subscribe-ask formula (mid-roll + outro)

Structure (4 slots, ~10-12s total):
1. Sincerity opener — "this genuinely matters to me…" (NOT guilt-trip tone)
2. Reason it helps — "it's what lets me keep testing these tools daily"
3. Social proof + honesty — "most of you watching aren't subscribed"
4. Concrete ask — like + subscribe + bell, in ONE sentence, then move on
   immediately to the next segment ("but with that… let's get into it").
   Their pattern: ask appears at ~90-120s, sometimes a second stylistic
   variation; outro repeats the full stack.
   Our defined scene: `purpose: "subscribe_ask"` in the script contract (see
   the `ai-news-long/script-director.md`) - this formula fills that scene.

## 6. Outro ritual (write ONCE, reuse every video - the habit builder)

Order:
1. Wrap + earned-value line ("hope this gave you something useful")
2. Links promise ("everything I showed is linked below")
3. Like + subscribe + bell (one sentence)
4. Community stack (mentions our anchors in fixed order)
5. Previously videos pointer
6. Warm sign-off ritual (fix a signature sign-off phrase + next-upload tease)

## 7. What NOT to copy (originality)

- Their exact phrases ("it would truly mean the world to me", "with that
  thought, guys", "spread positivity", "see you guys really shortly",
  "eliminate the noise").
- Their superlative lexicon as default praise (choose our own 3-5 praise words).
- Their sign-off. Write a new signature ritual.

## 8. Self-check (run in the reviewer pass)

- [ ] Hook teases >= 3 items within first 45s, none Fully explained before 0:50
- [ ] Mid-roll subscribe ask present ~90-120s, uses the 4-slot formula
- [ ] >= 1 rhetorical question per story block
- [ ] >= 1 flaw admission per demo section
- [ ] Every leak/rumor phrased with a source marker
- [ ] Narration lines for demo beats are screen-locked (VO matches what is
      visible at that timestamp - check vs storyboard)
- [ ] Zero copied phrases from the profile doc examples
- [ ] Outro ritual present and in the fixed order
